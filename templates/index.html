      Mission & Architecture

      About LoanLens & SDG 8 Impact

      Machine Learning applied towards transparent, equitable credit scoring and financial empowerment aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

      8

      UN SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth

      Goal 8.10 explicitly aims to "Strengthen the capacity of domestic financial institutions to encourage and expand access to banking, insurance and financial services for all."

      • Expanding Credit Access: Automated, low-latency credit assessments remove bureaucratic barriers for small businesses and first-time borrowers.
      • Objective Solvency Scoring: Replacing arbitrary manual discretion with transparent, mathematical risk models.
      • Financial Literacy Feedback: Generating explainable "Why?" reasons and actionable steps for rejected applicants to qualify in the future.

      End-to-End System Architecture

      How LoanLens transforms raw applicant parameters into actionable explainable underwriting decisions:

      1

      Data Ingestion

      3,000 synthesized credit profiles with multi-tier income, debt, and credit history.

      2

      Feature Engineering

      Synthesis of Total Income, Estimated EMI, Debt-to-Income (DTI), and Loan-to-Income (LTI).

      3

      Data Preprocessing

      Robust median imputations, StandardScaler normalization, and One-Hot encoding.

      4

      Multi-Model Training

      5 algorithms: Logistic Regression, KNN, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting.

      5

      Explainable AI (XAI)

      Model-agnostic marginal delta simulation quantifying exact feature impacts.

      6

      Real-Time Serving

      Flask microservice providing low-latency REST endpoints to this interactive UI.

      Ethical AI & Algorithmic Fairness Guarantee

      In adherence to ethical AI governance and anti-discrimination standards, protected demographic attributes including Gender, Religion, Caste, Race, and Marital Status are deliberately excluded from model training and feature sets. The underwriting assessment is strictly predicated on demonstrable financial solvency, debt-to-income sustainability, credit history, and asset parameters.
